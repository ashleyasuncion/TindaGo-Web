Everything your store needs

Three pillars built from real interviews with sari-sari store owners.

Stock Tracker

Color-coded status — Plenty, Low, or Out of Stock. Markup pricing helper. 120 pre-loaded Filipino products across 20 categories.

Daily Cash Summary

Record gastos and kita to auto-calculate profit and revolving fund. Net profit after store expenses. End-of-day closing routine.

Utang Notebook

Track customer debts, record payments, view full ledger history. Credit limit warnings. Send SMS receipts and reminders.

Guided Tutorial

Mandatory first-use walkthrough with 16 page-specific tutorials. Replayable anytime from the ? button. Designed for first-time smartphone users.

EN / Filipino

Full English and Filipino language support. Switch instantly in Settings. Designed for bilingual store owners.

SMS Notifications

Automated debt reminders via SMS. Send receipts to customers. One-tap messaging from the app. Quiet hours support.

System Requirements

How to Install

Since TindaGo is not on the Google Play Store, you'll need to install the APK manually.

1

Download the APK

Tap the "Download APK" button above to save the file to your phone.

2

Allow Unknown Sources

Go to Settings → Security → enable "Install from unknown sources" (or allow your browser to install APKs).

3

Install & Open

Open the downloaded APK file, tap Install, then open TindaGo. The guided tutorial will walk you through your first use!
